# Sample profiles

All people, companies and schools in these files are **fictional**, written for
this repository. Any resemblance to real people is coincidental.

- `contacts/`: people you want to meet at an event.
- `me/`: your own profile, used by the common-ground mode.

`amara_okafor.json` keeps a few fields the app must clean away (a photo URL,
an email address, empty values), to show the cleaning step.
